Shopify Daily Sales Report

Shopify Daily Sales Report is a small personal automation built on Salesforce. Every morning it reads the previous 24 hours of orders from a Shopify store, calculates a short sales summary (number of orders, order value and discounts), and emails that summary to the store owner.

How it uses Google

The app connects to one Gmail account that the developer owns. It uses the Gmail "send" permission only, so that the daily summary email is sent from that account. The app does not read, list, search or delete any email in the account.

Who uses it

This is a personal-use app. It is used by the developer and is not offered to the general public.
