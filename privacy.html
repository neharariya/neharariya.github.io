What this app does

Shopify Daily Sales Report is a personal automation. Once a day it sends a sales summary email from the developer's own Gmail account to the store owner.

Google user data the app accesses

  • Email address and basic profile (openid, email, profile): used only to identify which Google account was connected.
  • Gmail send permission (gmail.send): used only to send the daily summary email from the connected account.

The app does not read, list, search, modify or delete any email, contact or file in the Google account.

How data is stored

The OAuth access and refresh tokens that let the app send email are stored in the developer's own Salesforce organization, in a credential store protected by Salesforce. They are not copied anywhere else.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train machine-learning models. The only email content the app sends is the daily sales summary.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

The connected account can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the credential in Salesforce also removes the stored tokens.
